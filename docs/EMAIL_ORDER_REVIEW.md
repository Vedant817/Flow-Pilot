# Inbound email order handling

The classifier is an intent hint, **not permission to write**. The Gmail webhook checks every classified new order with `parseNewOrder`: explicit purchase wording, unambiguous structured product lines, and an explicit valid delivery date **and time** are required. The parser returns source evidence. It does not substitute today's date or an arbitrary time. Order documents use `gmail:<messageId>` as their unique idempotency key; the existing unique order index prevents a retry from creating another order.

Messages needing human attention become `email_reviews` records, deduplicated by `(orgId, gmailMessageId)`. The authenticated `/email-reviews` page (linked from Errors) shows the original message, reason and candidate changes; it supports resolving with a note, dismissing, or applying a **reviewed** amendment to a specified pending order. The apply action verifies the order's organization and sender, requires a supported explicit operation with a unique exact product match, blocks priced-order quantity changes, and commits the order, review status and rollup together in a MongoDB transaction. It fails closed without transaction support. Date-only changes require an explicit ISO or numeric date; vague terms such as “next Monday” remain for manual handling.

**Current coverage is deliberately narrow.** On the synthetic 80-email set, `npx tsx scripts/eval/write-policy.ts` reports **0/20 new orders ready for automatic creation** (11 have no structured product lines, seven no explicit purchase wording, and two missing a date or time). Five of the 20 update emails yield a supported change proposal. All other examples remain available in the review queue rather than being silently marked processed or written with guessed fields. Improve the parser against separately labeled real mail and rerun the write-policy audit before widening automation.

The review page does not submit arbitrary edits to orders. For unsupported changes, use the existing order-management process, then mark the review resolved with a note. `resolved` and `dismissed` items remain queryable via `/api/email-reviews?status=resolved|dismissed`; the open list is limited to 100 newest items.
